Super Ace

Golden Card

"Golden Card" will only appear on reels 2, 3, and 4.
When a "Golden Card" is eliminated, it flips and transforms into a "Joker Card" in the same position.
"Golden Cards" count as the same symbol as their regular cards for wins and elimination.

Joker Card

The "Joker Card" only appears after a "Golden Card" is eliminated.
The "Joker Card" comes in two types: "Big Joker Card" and "Little Joker Card".
When a "Big Joker Card" appears, the original "Golden Card" position becomes a "Big Joker Card", and additional "Big Joker Cards" are randomly copied to replace other symbols.
When a "Little Joker Card" appears, only the original "Golden Card" position becomes a "Little Joker Card".
"Joker Card" is a Wild and substitutes for all symbols except Scatter.

Combo Multiplier

In the Main Game, the "Combo Multiplier" has 4 levels: x1, x2, x3, and x5.
All wins are multiplied by the current "Combo Multiplier".
The "Combo Multiplier" starts at x1 for the first win of each spin.
After the 1st win in a spin, the "Combo Multiplier" increases to x2 for the 2nd win.
After the 2nd win in a spin, the "Combo Multiplier" increases to x3 for the 3rd win.
After the 3rd win in a spin, the "Combo Multiplier" increases to x5 for the 4th and all subsequent wins.
When there are no more wins, the "Combo Multiplier" resets to x1 and the next spin begins.

Game Rules
Super Ace is a video slot with 5 reels, 4 rows and 1024 ways.
Wins are awarded for 3 or more matching symbols on adjacent reels, starting from the leftmost reel.
During the game, all winning symbols will "flip" and be replaced by new symbols, which are then re-evaluated for more wins. When there are no more winning symbols, the total win for that round is awarded to the player.
The maximum win per spin is 10,000x the bet. Once this is reached, no further wins are evaluated and the winnings are settled.
Winning combinations and pays are made according to the paytable.
Game Interruption Mechanism

Malfunctions void all pays and plays. If a malfunction occurs during a game, the system will automatically complete the game and award the player.

If a disconnection occurs after receiving the player's bet and the player can no longer influence the outcome, the bet result remains valid.
