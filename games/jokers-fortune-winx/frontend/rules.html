Joker’s Fortune WinX

Five reels, three rows and five fixed paylines. Identical symbols pay consecutively from the leftmost reel. Only the highest prize on each line is paid; winning lines are added together.

The multiplier reel shows 1×, 2×, 3×, 4×, 5× or 10×. A regular play multiplies the middle-line win. Extra Play costs twice the base play and multiplies every winning line by its corresponding row multiplier.

Three, four or five Scatter symbols award 10×, 50× or 250× the base play. Scatter prizes are added separately.
