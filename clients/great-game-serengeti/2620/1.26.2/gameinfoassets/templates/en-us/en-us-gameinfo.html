FEATURES

HUNTING SEASON

When a Hunting Season symbol lands on the grid, a Hunt is triggered after all regular wins have been handled. One target is located above each reel. Five shots are fired from left to right that will either hit or miss the target.

Any Animal symbols on the reels that are hit are transformed into Coin or Trap symbols. Animal symbols display between 1 and 4 stars, which determines the minimum currency revealed.

1 Star Animal symbols grant a minimum of Bronze Coins.

2 Star Animal symbols grant a minimum of Silver Coins

3 Star Animal symbols grant a minimum of Gold Coins.

4 Star Animal symbols transform into a Trap symbol with a minimum value equal to Gold Coins.

All Coins are awarded as a multiple of your bet.

Bronze Coins have the following starting values: 0.2x, 0.4x, 0.6x, 0.8x, 1x

Silver Coins have the following starting values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x

Gold Coins have the following starting values: 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x

Diamond Coins have the following starting values: 100x, 150x, 250x, 500x

During a Hunt, when a 4 Star Animal symbol appears on a reel that is shot, that symbol will transform into a Trap symbol with a value. Trap symbols collect the values of all Coins and other Trap symbols and add the collected values to its existing Trap value. All collected symbols retain their original values when collected by a Trap symbol. Trap symbols activate top to bottom, left to right. The final win is the total value of all Coins and Trap symbols

If there is a Wild symbol on a reel that has been shot, that Wild symbol receives a Multiplier value. After all Coins and Trap symbols have been collected, each Wild symbol with a Multiplier multiplies between 1 and 5 Coin and Trap symbols. Wild symbols with Multipliers activate top to bottom, left to right. Wild symbols with Multipliers can have the following Multiplicative Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20.

One Hunting Season symbol can appear on the grid at the same time.

STAMPEDE REVEALS

During a spin, there is a chance that a Stampede Reveal will be triggered before any symbols have landed on the grid. When a Stampede Reveal is triggered, a Hunting Season symbol is guaranteed to land and no low-paying symbols can land on the grid. When the new symbols have landed, any wins and the Hunt will be handled before the next spin.

This bonus game keeps the base game’s mechanics, with a higher chance of landing Hunting Season symbols.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time in the bonus awards extra free spins:

  • 2 FS symbols award +2 free spins
  • 3 FS symbols award +4 free spins

BONUS GAME - ROARING REIGN

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game’s mechanics and Stampede Reveals will not trigger randomly. Instead, they are awarded through the Lion Bar, which is located outside of the grid

For every low-paying symbol located on a reel that is hit during a Hunt, the Lion Bar increases by one step. When the Lion Bar has increased by 10 steps, a Stampede Reveal is guaranteed to be triggered during the following spin. When the Stampede Reveal has been triggered, the Lion Bar resets before the next free spin. Any Lion Bar progression exceeding the amount required to trigger a Stampede Reveal, will be added to the Lion Bar, once it has been reset.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time in the bonus awards extra free spins:

  • 2 FS symbols award +2 free spins
  • 3 FS symbols award +4 free spins

HIDDEN EPIC BONUS - ZEBRAWL

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 5 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the mechanics of the ROARING REIGN bonus. In addition, a Hunting Season symbol is guaranteed to land on every spin.

FS symbols cannot land in this bonus.

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player.
