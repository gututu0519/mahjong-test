FEATURES

WILD EYEBALL SYMBOLS

When an EyeBall symbol lands, it can only land on the 4th row of the grid. Once the grid has settled, all EyeBall symbols shoot in 1 of 3 directions: straight up, diagonally left, or diagonally right. EyeBall symbols activate in order from left to right.

When an EyeBall shoots, every paying symbol along its path transforms into a Wild symbol. These transformed symbols can remain Wild or reveal an Additive Multiplier value. Wild symbols substitute for all symbols in the paytable. Multiplier values revealed on Wild symbols can be: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 200x.

If an EyeBall symbol hits an existing Wild symbol, that Wild symbol gains a Multiplier value. If an EyeBall symbol hits a Wild symbol with a Multiplier, the Multiplier value of that Wild will increase by adding a new Multiplier value to the existing Wild Multiplier. Multiplier values added to Wild symbols can be: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 200x.

Activated EyeBall symbols transform into Wild symbols. If more than 1 Wild Multiplier symbol is part of the same winning combination, the values are first added together before being applied to the win.

ENERGY FIELDS

There are 3 Energy Fields located on the left, top, and right edges of the grid. If an EyeBall symbol activates and hits an Energy Field, the EyeBall will bounce off the Energy Field and continue in another direction at the same angle. The EyeBall symbol will continue to transform paying symbols in its path into Wild symbols.

When an EyeBall symbol hits a Energy Field straight on, the EyeBall will bounce back the same way it came from, adding Multiplier values to the transformed Wild symbols. If an EyeBall hits an EyeBall symbol, it adds a Multiplier value.

Maximum win: {maximumWinMultiplier} times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
