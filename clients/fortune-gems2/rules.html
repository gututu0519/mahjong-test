Fortune Gems 2

Three reels, three rows and five fixed paylines. Three matching symbols on a payline win from left to right. The Wild substitutes for regular symbols.

The fourth reel multiplies line wins by 1, 2, 3, 5, 10 or 15. A Wheel symbol on its middle row triggers the Lucky Wheel.

Extra Bet costs 1.5 times the base bet. It removes the 1x reel multiplier and adds a multiplier to Lucky Wheel winnings.

Lucky Wheel prizes: 1, 3, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20, 30, 50, 100, 200 or 1000 times the base bet.

Paytable

Three matching symbols on one payline, before the special reel multiplier. Values are multiples of the base bet.
