SUPER CASCADES

When a winning combination occurs, all regular paying symbols of the type that were part of the winning combination are removed from the grid, and new ones drop down to fill the empty spaces.

GOLDEN SQUARES

When a winning combination occurs, the squares behind the winning symbols will be highlighted as Golden Squares.

When a Rainbow symbol lands on the grid, it activates any Golden Squares after all wins have been collected. Activated Golden Squares reveal Special symbols that are either Bronze, Silver, Gold or Diamond Coins and can also reveal Green or Gold Clover symbols and Bag of Gold or Pot of Gold symbols.

When an Epic Rainbow symbol lands, all grid positions are highlighted as Golden Squares and reveal Special symbols. The reveal and activation process is the same for both Rainbow symbols. Epic Rainbow symbols also have a chance to land and trigger a Blood Rain, where all grid positions are highlighted as Blood Squares. Blood Squares keeps the mechanics of Golden Squares, but will not reveal any Bronze Coin symbols.

All Coins are cash prizes that are paid as a multiple of your bet.

  • Bronze Coins can have the following values: 0.2x, 0.4x, 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x
  • Silver Coins can have the following values: 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x
  • Gold Coins can have the following values: 20x, 30x, 40x, 50x
  • Diamond Coins can have the following values: 100x, 150x, 250x, 500x

After all Golden Squares have been revealed, any Clover symbols will activate. Green Clover symbols will multiply all Coins or existing Bag of Gold or Pot of Gold symbols on adjacent squares with one of the following Multiplicative Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20.

Gold Clover symbols will multiply all Coins or existing Bag of Gold or Pot of Gold symbols on the grid with one of the following Multiplicative Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20. If there are no Special symbols near the Clover symbols, they will activate once there are Special symbols on the grid to multiply.

Once all Clover symbols have applied their Multipliers, Bag of Gold symbols will activate. When Bag of Gold symbols activate, they collect the values of all Coins and other activated Bag of Gold and Pot of Gold symbols on adjacent 3x3 squares and store their total value. Bag of Gold symbols activate top to bottom, left to right.

Golden Squares will reveal new Special symbols once they have been collected by the Bag of Gold symbol, and the process above repeats. If a Bag of Gold symbol has already been activated, it will not collect again.

Once all Bag of Gold symbols have activated, any Pot of Gold symbols present on the grid will activate. When Pot of Gold symbols activate, they collect the values of all Coins and other Bag of Gold and Pot of Gold symbols, and store their total value. Pot of Gold symbols activate top to bottom, left to right. Golden Squares will reveal new Special symbols once they have been collected by the Pot of Gold symbol, and the process above repeats. If a Pot of Gold symbol has already been activated, it will not collect again.

Once all reveals and activations have been completed, all Coins, Bag of Gold and Pot of Gold symbols will be added together and multiplied by your bet amount to determine the final awarded win. Once all the wins and cash prizes have been awarded, all Special symbols and Golden Squares are removed from the grid for the next spin.

BONUS GAME - COFFIN BREAK

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game’s mechanics and all Golden and Blood Squares remain highlighted until either Rainbow symbol activates them.

When a high-paying symbol is part of a winning combination, the win is handled before all symbols of the same symbol type are upgraded to Epic symbols for the remainder of the bonus, including any new symbols that land. If there are multiple winning combinations including the same high-paying symbol, the symbol type will be upgraded after the first win has been handled. Wins are handled from left to right.

In addition, a Symbol Bar is located outside of the grid. For every symbol type that is upgraded to an Epic symbol, a new Level is reached on the Symbol Bar:

  • Level 1 awards 2 additional free spins.
  • Level 2 awards 2 additional free spins.
  • Level 3 awards 2 additional free spins.
  • Level 4 awards 2 additional free spins and guarantees that 1 Rainbow or Epic Rainbow symbol will land on the next spin.

When an Epic symbol is part of a win, the square behind the winning symbol is highlighted as a Blood Square.

BONUS GAME - LOVE AT FIRST BITE

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the mechanics of the COFFIN BREAK bonus and all Golden and Blood Squares remain highlighted even after being activated by a Rainbow symbol. When Epic Rainbow symbols activate, only the Golden and Blood Squares that were on the grid before will remain after the full grid activation is complete.

For every Level that is reached on the Symbol Bar, +2 free spins are awarded.

HIDDEN EPIC BONUS - DO YOUR FANG

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 5 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the mechanics of the LOVE AT FIRST BITE bonus, with a Rainbow or Epic Rainbow symbol guaranteed to land on every spin. In addition, when Level 4 is reached on the Symbol Bar, an Epic Rainbow symbol is guaranteed to land on the next spin.

All Coins that are revealed during the bonus display a value of at least 1x.

For every Level that is reached on the Symbol Bar, +2 free spins are awarded.

Maximum win: {maximumWinMultiplier} times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player.
