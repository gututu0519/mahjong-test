FEATURES

EXPANDING WICKED GRIN COINS

When a Wicked Grin or Epic Wicked Grin symbol is located on the bottom row, it will expand after all wins are handled. Expanded Wicked Grin and Epic Wicked Grin symbols will transform all symbols on that reel into Coins or a Multiplier.

All Coins are paid as a multiple of your bet.

Bronze Coins have the following starting values: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x

Silver Coins have the following starting values: 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x

Gold Coins have the following starting values: 10x, 20x, 30x, 40x, 50x

Diamond Coins have the following starting values: 100x, 150x, 250x, 500x

Multipliers have the following values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20

When a Multiplier is revealed, it will add a Multiplier value to all Coins above it and to Coins on the adjacent reels on rows above the Multiplier. If more than one Multiplier is revealed, they will add their values to the Coins one at a time from left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Multipliers cannot appear on the top row.

Expanded Epic Wicked Grin symbols trigger any type of Wicked Grin symbols on the grid to expand from the position they are in.

Only one type of Wicked Grin symbol can land on a reel at a time.

SPREADING FS SYMBOLS

When at least 1 FS or Super FS scatter symbol is located on the bottom row, each FS symbol expands across the entire reel it lands on after all wins have been awarded. Every position the FS and Super FS symbol covers represents a certain number of free spins. The total number of free spins awarded for the bonus is determined by adding up all the numbers on positions covered by each FS or Super FS symbol. FS and Super FS symbols can have the following values: 1, 2, 3.

FS and Super FS cannot be on the grid at the same time. There can be a maximum of 2 FS and Super FS symbols on the grid at the same time.

BONUS GAME - STASH N STACK

Activate this bonus with 5-30 free spins by landing at least 1 expanding FS scatter symbol during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game’s mechanics. In addition, above each reel is a Stash with Multipliers starting at x1. When Wicked Grin symbols expand, all Coin values on that reel are multiplied by the Multiplier in the Stash. That Stash Multiplier will then increase by 1 for the next free spin.

Landing additional expanding FS symbols in the bonus awards extra free spins. Each expanding FS symbol awards at least 5 free spins and max 15 free spins. The total additional free spins awarded can be between 5-30.

BONUS GAME - COIN CRAZE

Activate this bonus with 5-30 free spins by landing at least 1 expanding Super FS scatter symbol during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game’s mechanics. In addition, above each reel is a Coin Collector which starts at 1. When any Wicked Grin symbol expands, all Coin values on that reel are saved in the Coin Collector until the end of the bonus. When Multipliers are revealed during an expansion, the Multiplier affects the Coin values and multiplies the saved value in the Coin Collector on that reel and on the adjacent reels. At the end of the bonus, all Coin Collector values are added together and multiplied by the bet amount to determine the final awarded win.

Landing additional expanding Super FS symbols in the bonus awards extra free spins. Each expanding Super FS symbol awards at least 5 free spins and max 15 free spins. The total additional free spins awarded can be between 5-30.

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
