3 Billion Vaults

5 reels · 3 rows · 243 ways. Matching regular symbols pay from left to right, starting on the first reel. Only the longest match pays for each way. Wins on different ways are added together.

Moving Wild

Wild substitutes for regular symbols. In the main game, Wild appears on reels 3–5, remains on the board and moves up one position on the next spin. Each play amount keeps its own Wild positions.

Heist Bag

Bill symbols may appear over regular symbols on the first and fifth reels. Matching bill symbols on both reels award a Heist Bag prize: 3.8, 8.8, 18.8, 38.8, 88.8, 188.8, 388.8 or 888.8 times the play amount.

Vaults and Free Spins

Diamond symbols fly into the matching colored vault and may trigger its feature. Up to three vault colors can trigger together. Vault collection animations do not indicate the chance of triggering a feature. Features start with 6 free spins at the triggering play amount.

Free-spin Wilds appear on reels 2–5. Red diamonds can award cash of 0.5, 1 or 1.5 times the play amount, extra spins, or a Jackpot Pick feature when applicable.

Jackpot Pick

Choose from 15 cards. Three matching jackpot cards award that prize. Reveal three Upgrade cards before completing a jackpot to upgrade it one level. After five seconds without a selection, cards are selected automatically. Jackpot multipliers: MINI 15×, MINOR 30×, MAJOR 100×, GRAND 1,000× and FORTUNE 5,000×.

Play Feature and Extra Bet

Play Feature offers MIGHTY, MEGA and SUPER at 23×, 46.5× and 100× the selected play amount. The feature uses that selected play amount. Extra Bet costs 1.5× and increases the opportunity to trigger a feature. Selecting a feature purchase turns Extra Bet off.
